# ResearchFlow AI - NextGen Resume AI Case Study

## 1. Context & Business Goal
**Company**: NextGen Resume AI  
**Industry**: B2C SaaS / Career Tech  
**Objective**: Fall Campus Recruiting: Acquire 1,000 university seniors and junior software engineers looking for high-paying tech roles.  
**Target Audience**: College seniors in Computer Science/Engineering, bootcamp graduates, and career changers.  
**Competitors Analyzed**: Novoresume, Kickresume, Teal, Rezi.

---

## 2. Evidence Extracted & Conflicts Flagged

### Extracted Evidence Highlights:
- **Novoresume Claim**: Starter plan is \$19/month billed annually; \$29/month billed monthly. Free plan capped at 1-page PDF.
- **Kickresume Claim**: \$19/month annual tier; AI keyword matching requires Pro subscription.
- **Positioning Insight**: Competitors emphasize visual design templates over recruiter-vetted ATS scoring algorithms.

### Conflict Flagged & Resolved:
- **Conflict Item**: Novoresume Pricing Discrepancy (\$19 vs \$29).
- **Resolution**: Operator flagged as `HUMAN_VERIFIED` explaining the 30% discount on annual commitments versus monthly billing.
