## 1. The Core Brand Symbol: *The Convergence Vector*

```
          [ SIGNAL 1: CROWN OF R & UPPER HORIZONTAL ]
          ══════════════════════════════════════════╮
                                                    │
          [ SIGNAL 2: CENTRAL STREAM & F-CROSSBAR ] │  [ THE SYNTHESIS CORE ]
          ════════════════════                      │
                                                    │
          [ SIGNAL 3: BASE STREAM ]                 │
          ═══════════════                           │
                         ╲                          │
                          ╲                         ╰────────╮
                           ╲                                 │
                            ╲                                │
                             ╰───────────────────────────────┴────▶ [ FORWARD EXECUTION VECTOR ]
```

### Visual Metaphor: "Multiple Signals $\rightarrow$ Convergence $\rightarrow$ One Forward Intelligent Form"
1. **Multiple Signal Inflow (Left)**:
   - Three horizontal stream paths representing independent market inputs (pricing structures, feature matrices, web evidence).
2. **The Intelligence Core & Synthesis (Center Loop)**:
   - The upper stream executes a mathematical arc, forming the upper loop of an **R** and the top horizontal beam of an **F**, where raw signals converge into structured intelligence.
3. **The Forward Execution Vector (Right Diagonal & Arrow)**:
   - All three streams unify into a single, dominant $45^\circ$ cantilevered execution vector surging down-right, representing high-velocity momentum and verified action.
4. **Subtle Hidden Typography**:
   - The geometry naturally embeds the letterforms **R** (the upper loop + dynamic leg) and **F** (the top bar + middle crossbar + vertical alignment) without cartoonish literalism.

## 2. Monochrome Purity & Scale Calibration

The symbol is engineered on a strict geometric grid to guarantee flawless legibility across all scales:
- **Solid Monochrome (100% Black / 100% White)**: The three distinct horizontal inflow channels and the decisive forward vector maintain crisp optical contrast without needing color gradients.
- **Favicon Scale ($16\times 16\text{px}$ / $32\times 32\text{px}$)**: Stroke weights and negative-space apertures are balanced so the silhouette remains sharp in browser tabs next to global tech brands (Stripe, Linear, Notion, Vercel).
- **Hero & Billboard Scale ($512\text{px} - 1024\text{px}$)**: Mathematical curvature tangencies and $45^\circ$ beveled facets reveal high-precision architectural software craft.

## 5. Typography Hierarchy

1. **Display Wordmark**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Weight 800 ExtraBold, tight kerning `-0.03em`).
2. **Body & Interface**: *Inter* (Weights 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold).
3. **Data & Citations**: *JetBrains Mono* (Weights 500 Medium, 700 Bold).

## 6. Clear Space & Minimum Sizing

- **Clear Space**: Minimum $0.5X$ (where $X$ is the height of the upper loop).
- **Minimum Digital Size**:
  - Horizontal Logo: $130\text{px}$ width
  - Standalone Symbol: $18\text{px} \times 18\text{px}$
  - Favicon: $16\text{px} \times 16\text{px}$
