# ResearchFlow AI - User Guide & Operator Walkthrough

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating Your Workspace
1. Navigate to the application at `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Click **Sign Up** to create a private workspace.
3. Enter your Name, Email, Password, and your Product / Business Name (e.g. *DevFlow AI*).
4. Your private workspace is instantly provisioned in an **honest clean state** ready for your first research run.

---

## 2. Launching a Research Mission

1. Click **+ New Research Job** in the top navigation or on the Research page.
2. Fill out the mission parameters:
   - **Business Name**: Your product under analysis.
   - **Product Description**: A concise 1–2 sentence overview of your value proposition.
   - **Campaign Objective**: What you want to achieve (e.g. *"Fall Hiring Sprint: Acquire 500 junior engineers"*).
   - **Target Audience**: Who you are selling to (e.g. *"College seniors and bootcamp grads"*).
   - **Competitor URLs**: Add 2–4 public landing, feature, or pricing pages.
3. Click **Launch Autonomous Research**.

---

## 3. Reviewing Pipeline Progress

Watch the live stage progression:
1. `VALIDATING`: Checking URLs and input formats.
2. `RESEARCHING`: Fetching and cleaning visible page text.
3. `EXTRACTING`: Parsing structured claims into Evidence.
4. `NORMALIZING`: Checking for cross-source conflicts.
5. `ANALYZING`: Synthesizing positioning gaps and opportunities.
6. `GENERATING`: Drafting campaign brief and channel assets.
7. `AWAITING_REVIEW`: Ready for operator sign-off.

---

## 4. Inspecting Evidence & Resolving Conflicts

- **Evidence Explorer**: Filter claims by `Pricing`, `Product Features`, `Target Audience`, and `Positioning`. Inspect direct supporting quotes and confidence scores.
- **Conflict Center**: If competitors have conflicting pricing (e.g. $19 vs $29), click **Resolve Conflict**, select **Human Verified** or **Dismissed**, and add an operator note.

---

## 5. Campaign Approval & Task Management

1. In the **Campaign Strategy Hub**, review the synthesized Executive Summary, Core Problem, Lead Positioning Angle, and Primary Message.
2. Open the **Visual Ad Creative Studio** to preview and customize live LinkedIn, Google Search, and X/Twitter ad creative mockups.
3. Click **Approve Campaign** to move the brief to `APPROVED`.
4. Navigate to the **Execution Tasks** board to track the generated action items (e.g. *"Update landing page positioning"*, *"Review and schedule LinkedIn post"*).
